## Findings

1. **ea89377, confirmed.** `crates/vitality_abilities/src/tasks.rs` `on_child_settled`: the "parent still Running" guard is skipped when the parent is the ability, on the claim that an ended ability ignores `EndAbility`. A root `Composite::Sequence` also calls `start_child`, though. `CompleteState::on_enter` queues `TaskCompleted` before `ChildSettled`, so a `TaskCompleted` listener that cancels the ability runs first, and Recovery stops only the Running children. Then `ChildSettled(A)` arrives with the same cast and activates the Pending `B` during cooldown. Repro: root `Composite::Sequence` over `[#A InstantTask, #B LongTask]`, with a `TaskCompleted` observer that cancels the ability when A completes. The ability ends in Recovery, B is Running and B is in `Starts`. Expected: B never starts once the ability has left Active. Queued as T-115.
