## Owner must see — two library defects, out of this loop's scope, reported not fixed

1. **Cross-actor staleness: 3 resolver passes.** A plain base write on a source reaches a reader three
   passes later (two with a hand-triggered `MarkNodeDirty`), because the write is marked only in
   `EffectsSet::Notify`, chained after `UpdateCurrentValues` (`vitality_core/src/lib.rs:54-64`,
   `vitality_effects/src/systems.rs:272-283`). At 60 Hz that is two frames of stale derived stats behind
   every buff whose source moves. The µs/tick column is throughput, not latency.
2. **`are_different` is always true for floats.** `vitality_core/src/math.rs:32-33`:
   `self.abs_diff(rhs) > Self::Output::MIN` where `Output` is `f32`/`f64`, so the threshold is
   `f32::MIN` (negative) and `abs_diff ≥ 0` — always true; NaN is the only false case. Chain:
   `attributes.rs:326` → `update_attribute` always writes `set_current_value` → `systems.rs:272-283`
   `Changed<T>` → `MarkNodeDirty` → `systems.rs:32-42` re-inserts `Dirty<T>`. Measured consequence: one
   marked actor still had `dirty_entity_count == 1` after 50 further ticks — **a dirty actor never becomes
   idle.** That single line is why `floor::prepare_floor` exists, why `allocs_per_tick` reads its
   denominator before the window, why P-15's timed unit must move the source, and it makes every "dirty"
   column a world that never converges. Any epsilon fix must also say what NaN should do.
